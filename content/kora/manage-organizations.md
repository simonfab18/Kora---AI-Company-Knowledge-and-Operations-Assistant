## Create and switch

Owners can create up to three active organizations. Use the workspace switcher to move between organizations where you have access.

## Share access

Invite a new email or add a directory member to another organization you manage. Roles apply independently in each organization.

## Delete carefully

Organization deletion removes related workspace data. Kora requires the exact organization confirmation phrase before the destructive action can continue.
