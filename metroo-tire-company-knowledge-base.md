# Metroo Tire Company Knowledge Base

## Company Overview

Metroo Tire Company is a regional tire retailer and service provider specializing in passenger vehicles, light trucks, commercial vans, and small fleet accounts. The company sells new tires, performs installation and balancing, offers wheel alignment, tire rotation, puncture repair, seasonal tire changeovers, and tire safety inspections.

Our goal is to help customers choose the right tires, maintain them safely, and understand when replacement or repair is required.

## Core Services

### Tire Sales

Metroo sells tires for sedans, SUVs, pickup trucks, vans, and light commercial vehicles. Tire recommendations should consider vehicle type, driving conditions, mileage expectations, weather, load rating, speed rating, and customer budget.

### Tire Installation

Standard installation includes mounting the tire onto the wheel, valve stem replacement when applicable, inflation to the correct pressure, wheel balancing, and installation on the vehicle using manufacturer torque specifications.

### Wheel Balancing

Wheel balancing reduces vibration caused by uneven tire and wheel weight distribution. Customers may need balancing if they feel steering wheel vibration at highway speeds, uneven tire wear, or shaking after a tire replacement.

### Wheel Alignment

Alignment adjusts suspension angles so the tires meet the road correctly. Alignment is recommended after suspension repairs, after hitting a major pothole or curb, when tires show uneven wear, or when the vehicle pulls to one side.

### Tire Rotation

Tire rotation helps tires wear evenly. For most vehicles, Metroo recommends rotation every 5,000 to 7,500 miles unless the vehicle manufacturer specifies a different interval.

### Puncture Repair

A puncture may be repairable if it is located in the tread area, is no larger than 1/4 inch in diameter, and the tire has not been driven while flat. Sidewall punctures, shoulder damage, internal damage, and tires with insufficient tread depth are not repairable.

## Customer Service Standards

### Greeting Customers

Employees should greet every customer within 30 seconds of arrival. The greeting should be friendly, direct, and service-focused.

Example greeting:

"Welcome to Metroo Tire. What vehicle are we helping you with today?"

### Information To Collect

Before recommending a tire or service, employees should collect:

- Customer name
- Phone number
- Vehicle year, make, and model
- Tire size if available
- Current issue or customer goal
- Typical driving conditions
- Budget range
- Timeline or urgency

### Recommendation Standard

Employees should offer a good, better, and best option when possible. Each option should explain the tradeoff in plain language, such as mileage warranty, wet traction, ride comfort, winter performance, road noise, and price.

### Pricing Transparency

Quotes should include tire price, installation, balancing, valve stems if applicable, disposal fees, taxes, and any optional protection plan. Employees should avoid quoting only the tire price when the customer is asking for the total installed cost.

## Tire Recommendation Guide

### All-Season Tires

All-season tires are suitable for drivers who want year-round convenience in mild climates. They are designed for dry roads, wet roads, and light snow, but they are not a replacement for winter tires in severe winter conditions.

### Touring Tires

Touring tires focus on comfort, quiet ride quality, and longer tread life. They are commonly recommended for sedans, crossovers, and family vehicles.

### Performance Tires

Performance tires provide stronger handling, steering response, and grip. They may wear faster than touring tires and may be less comfortable on rough roads.

### All-Terrain Tires

All-terrain tires are recommended for trucks and SUVs that split time between paved roads and gravel, dirt, job sites, or mild off-road conditions. They may produce more road noise than highway tires.

### Winter Tires

Winter tires are recommended when temperatures regularly fall below 45 degrees Fahrenheit or when customers drive in snow, ice, or slush. Winter tires use softer rubber compounds and tread designs that improve cold-weather traction.

### Commercial Van Tires

Commercial van tires should be selected based on load rating, durability, and service use. Employees must confirm the tire load index meets or exceeds the vehicle requirement.

## Tire Safety Standards

### Minimum Tread Depth

Metroo recommends tire replacement at 4/32 inch for wet-weather safety and requires replacement at 2/32 inch because the tire is legally worn out in many jurisdictions.

### Uneven Wear

Uneven tire wear may indicate alignment problems, suspension issues, improper inflation, lack of rotation, or worn steering components. Employees should recommend an inspection when uneven wear is visible.

### Sidewall Damage

A tire with sidewall bulges, bubbles, cuts exposing cords, or punctures in the sidewall must be replaced. Sidewall damage is not repairable.

### Driving On A Flat Tire

If a tire has been driven while flat or severely underinflated, it may have internal sidewall damage even if the outside appears normal. Employees should inspect the inside of the tire before approving repair.

### Tire Pressure

Tire pressure should be set according to the vehicle placard, usually located on the driver-side door jamb. Employees should not use the maximum pressure printed on the tire sidewall as the normal inflation target.

## Warranty Policy

### Manufacturer Warranty

Most tire warranties are provided by the tire manufacturer, not Metroo. Warranty coverage may include treadwear mileage, workmanship, and materials, depending on the brand and model.

### Warranty Requirements

Customers may need proof of purchase, rotation records, alignment records, and evidence that the tires were maintained properly. Lack of maintenance records may reduce or deny warranty coverage.

### Road Hazard Protection

Road hazard protection is optional unless included in a specific promotion. It may cover eligible damage from potholes, nails, glass, and road debris. It does not cover vandalism, racing, off-road abuse, collision damage, or tires driven after air loss.

### Warranty Claim Process

1. Inspect the tire and record the customer complaint.
2. Confirm purchase history and service records.
3. Measure tread depth at multiple points.
4. Photograph visible damage or wear patterns.
5. Contact the manufacturer or distributor if required.
6. Explain the decision and any prorated credit clearly to the customer.

## Appointment And Workflow SOP

### Booking An Appointment

When booking an appointment, employees should confirm the customer name, phone number, vehicle, requested service, preferred time, tire availability, and expected service duration.

### Check-In Process

At check-in, employees should verify the service request, inspect tire condition, record mileage, confirm wheel lock key availability if needed, and review the estimate before work begins.

### Service Completion

Before returning the vehicle, technicians should confirm lug nuts are torqued to specification, tire pressure is set correctly, old tires are disposed of or loaded as requested, and the invoice matches the approved service.

### Customer Handoff

Employees should explain what was done, note any safety concerns, recommend next service timing, and invite the customer to return for pressure checks or rotations.

## Common Customer Questions

### How often should I rotate my tires?

Most customers should rotate tires every 5,000 to 7,500 miles. Some vehicles have different requirements, especially staggered tire setups or directional tires.

### Can you repair a nail in my tire?

A nail puncture may be repairable if it is in the tread area, smaller than 1/4 inch, and the tire was not driven flat. Sidewall and shoulder punctures are not repairable.

### Why does my car shake after getting new tires?

Shaking after tire service is often caused by wheel imbalance, bent wheels, uneven tire wear, or suspension problems. Metroo should inspect the balance first, then check wheels and suspension if vibration continues.

### Do I need an alignment with new tires?

An alignment is recommended if the old tires show uneven wear, the vehicle pulls, the steering wheel is off-center, or suspension work was recently performed. Alignment protects the customer’s tire investment.

### What tire pressure should I use?

Use the pressure listed on the vehicle placard, not the maximum pressure printed on the tire sidewall.

### Are winter tires worth it?

Winter tires are worth it for customers who regularly drive in snow, ice, slush, or temperatures below 45 degrees Fahrenheit. They improve cold-weather grip and stopping performance.

## Escalation Rules

Employees should involve a manager when:

- A customer disputes a warranty decision
- A customer claims vehicle damage after service
- A fleet customer requests custom pricing
- A tire failure may involve safety risk
- A customer asks for a repair that violates Metroo safety standards
- A refund request exceeds normal store authority

## Do Not Say To Customers

Employees should not say:

- "This tire is definitely covered" before warranty review
- "You can keep driving on it" if sidewall damage or severe wear is present
- "The tire pressure on the sidewall is the right pressure"
- "A plug-only repair is fine" for a permanent repair
- "Alignment is always required" when there is no evidence or recommendation basis
